Our story

From a home kitchen to your celebration table

Founded in 2021

Fairyland Cake Co. grew out of a passion for artisan baking — starting as a boutique home kitchen and growing into a trusted local name for custom cakes, whimsical cupcakes and detailed dessert spreads for birthdays and events.

Mission & vision

To craft high-quality, handcrafted baked goods that add charm to special celebrations, while building a reliable local brand identity that customers can trust for every occasion.

Who we bake for

Local event hosts, parents planning birthdays, couples preparing for their big day, and community members seeking artisan baked goods that feel personal.

✿

What guides every order

Customisation first

Every cake is fully tailored — flavour, size, dietary needs (gluten-free, eggless) and theme are designed around your celebration.

Clear communication

Structured order forms replace chaotic DMs, so nothing about your event gets lost or delayed.

Honest pricing

Base prices and ordering timelines are published up front — no surprises when your invoice arrives.
